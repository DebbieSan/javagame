Welcome to the Adventures of Alice in Wonderland. 

This is a text-based adventure game. The objective of this game is to collect points, save bunnies and get to the end without losing all your energy. 

In this adventure, you are Alice, a young girl who gets lost in the wacky world of Wonderland. You will visit many locations to collect items that will help you throughout your adventure. 

But be careful, some of these locations have mean spirited folks who will try to drain all your energy before you can reach the finish line!

In each location, you will find different items and if you choose, you may collect them and enter them on your inventory.

Additionally, you will find a safe room where you can leave your items safely and also restore your energy.

To move from one room to another, perform actions such as pick item, you will use text commands. These text commands should be types as verb-noun combinations separated by a space.

If at any point you wish to see these instructions again, please enter "i", lower case, on your keyboard.
