Welcome to the Adventures of Alice in Wonderland. 

This is a text-based adventure game. The objective of this game is to collect enough of the Mad Hatter's coins, save enough bunnies, which are the White Rabbit's little friends, and get to The Tree without losing all your energy. 

In this adventure, you are Alice, a young girl who gets lost in the wacky world of Wonderland. You will visit many locations to collect items that will help you throughout your adventure. 

But be careful, some of these locations have mean spirited folks who will try to drain all your energy before you can reach the finish line! Being Wonderland, you can't predict where they will be, or if they will still be there if you leave eand return. You should try talking to them when you get the chance.

In each location, you will find different items and if you choose, you may collect them and enter them on your inventory.

Additionally, you will find a safe room where you can leave your items safely and also restore your energy.

To move from one room to another, perform actions such as grab item, you will use text commands. These text commands should be types as verb-noun combinations separated by a space. type "open help" to see more.

On the topic of moving rooms: going from room to room can require a lot of energy. Be sure to check your energy levels often, as you may starve! Type "open energy" to see your health.

if you wish to see further instructions about the commmands you use, please type "open help" with your keyboard and press enter/return.
