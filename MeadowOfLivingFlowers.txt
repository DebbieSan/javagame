Welcome to the Meadow of Living Flowers!

The Meadow of Living Flowers is a place where Alice stumbles upon while chasing the White Rabbit after shrinking to the size of an insect. While Alice was following the White Rabbit while the size of an ant she finds herself unable to catch him while she was that small. Then she noticed the most peculiar butterflies, but someone corrects her, saying that they are 'bread-and-butterflies.'

This place may seem pretty and all but be aware, the flowers are not as friendly as they seem and they will turn against you because they think you are a weed!

How fast can you leave this room?
