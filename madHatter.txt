A small, impolite hatter who lives in perpetual tea-time. The Mad Hatter enjoys frustrating Alice.
"Have you seen my watch?", asks the Mad Hatter. "It's nearly tea time!"
"By the by, why is a raven like a writing-desk?", asked the Hatter.
"Have you no idea? Well, that's a shame. I was hoping you might know, as I certainly don't!"
