The co-ruler of Wonderland. The King is ineffectual and generally unlikeable, but lacks the Queen’s ruthlessness and undoes her orders of execution.
"There was a Cheshire Cat looking at me not long ago, and I didn't like it one bit", says the King.
"He also declined to kiss my hand. Can you believe it? So impertinent!", the King continued.
"You may claim you read in a book that a cat has a right to look at me so, but I didn't read this book. No no, the cat must be removed if he comes back!", declared the King.
